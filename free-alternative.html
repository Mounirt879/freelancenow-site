A free alternative to service apps

Many apps that connect you with local help charge someone along the way: a service fee for the client, a fee per lead or a commission for the worker. FreelanceNow takes nothing. Here is how it compares, so you can choose.

FreelanceNow
Who pays FreelanceNowNobody. Free for clients and for workers.
Commission on the jobNone. The worker keeps everything.
Fee per contact or leadNone.
How you find someoneYou see who is available right now near you, and they call you.
How you payDirectly to the person, as you agreed.

What to compare when you choose an app

  • Total cost for you, including service or booking fees
  • What the worker keeps after fees or commission
  • How fast someone can actually come
  • Whether you can talk to the person before you agree
  • Which jobs and which countries are covered

We only describe FreelanceNow here. Other apps change their prices and rules: check their own pages before you compare.
