How FreelanceNow works

A live map of people near you who can take a job now, whatever the job. You see who is free, you call, you agree together.

If you need someone

  1. Say what you needAny job, for an hour, a day or longer.
  2. See who is freeWhat they do, their rate, how fast they usually answer.
  3. Call or textYou agree on the job and pay the person directly.

If you can take jobs

  1. Free profileYour job, your area, your rate.
  2. One switchAvailable now, or regular hours like every Sunday or evenings 6 to 10 pm.
  3. Direct callsNo fee per contact, no commission, no contract.
