What you can ask for

  • Regular home cleaning
  • Deep clean of kitchen and bathrooms
  • Move-in or move-out cleaning
  • Cleaning after a party or renovation

What to tell them

  • Size of the place and number of rooms
  • What matters most: kitchen, bathroom, windows
  • Whether products and equipment are on site
  • Pets in the home

Before they start

  • Agree on hours and the rate before
  • Say what is included and what is not
  • Look at the reviews
  • Keep valuables put away, as with anyone new
