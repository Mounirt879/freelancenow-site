Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. No fee per contact, no commission, no subscription.

Do I need a company?

No. You work the way you already do and agree on everything directly with the customer.

What if I am busy?

Switch to not available in one tap. And every two days the app asks if you are still available: if you say no or don't answer, you leave the map after one more day.

Can I set regular hours?

Yes. For example every Sunday, or every evening from 6 to 10 pm. During those hours you show as available and people can call you directly.

Can I offer more than one job?

Yes. Add the main one now, you can add others in the app.
