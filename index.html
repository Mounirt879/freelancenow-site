Questions

Is FreelanceNow free?

Yes, for both sides. No fee per contact, no commission, no subscription.

What kind of jobs can I find?

Any job: babysitter tonight, a waiter for a shift, a truck driver tomorrow, a plumber, an HR consultant, a photographer, and anything else people offer near you.

How do I pay the person?

Directly, the way you agree together. FreelanceNow is not in the middle.

How does availability work?

People switch themselves to available when they can take a job. They stay on the map for two days, then the app asks if they are still available: yes keeps them on, no or no answer takes them off. People can also set regular hours, like every Sunday or every evening from 6 to 10 pm.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. FreelanceNow works everywhere, in English, Spanish, French and Arabic. The more people around you join, the fuller the map gets.

I can take jobs. How do I join?

Get the app, add your job and your area, then switch to available. It takes two minutes and it is free.
