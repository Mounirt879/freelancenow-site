What you can ask for

  • An evening out, from a few hours to midnight
  • After-school pickup and homework
  • A weekend afternoon
  • Help during a busy week

What to tell them

  • Ages of the children and how many
  • Start and end time
  • Bedtime routine, meals, allergies
  • Your area

Before they start

  • Call and talk before you agree
  • Read the reviews and ask about experience
  • Agree on the hourly rate and how they get home
  • Leave your numbers and emergency contacts

How it works

  1. Say what you needOne line is enough: the job, when, where.
  2. See who is freePeople near you who switched to available right now, with reviews.
  3. Talk and agreeThey call you. You agree on the price together. No fee, no commission.

FreelanceNow puts people in touch. Price, skills and paperwork are agreed directly between you, so take a minute to call, read the reviews and agree before the job starts.

Questions

How do I know the babysitter is serious?

Read the profile and the reviews from other families, call before you agree, and meet them first if you can. Trust your judgment.

Can I find someone for tonight?

Yes, if someone near you is available tonight. People who set evenings as regular hours show up too.

How much does it cost?

The babysitter sets the rate. FreelanceNow is free for families and sitters.
