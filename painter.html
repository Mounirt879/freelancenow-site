What you can ask for

  • Repainting a room or a whole apartment
  • Doors, trims and ceilings
  • Touch-ups before moving out
  • Exterior: fence, gate, shutters

What to tell them

  • Rooms and approximate surface
  • Colors chosen and who buys the paint
  • Condition of the walls: holes, cracks, damp
  • Dates and whether the room is empty

Before they start

  • Ask for a fixed price for the whole job
  • Agree on who protects floors and furniture
  • Look at photos of past work
  • Check the finish in daylight before paying

How it works
